# TimeTable Domain Glossary

Weekly academic timetable and schedule manager for KTU Computer Science students and faculty.

## Language

**Period**:
A scheduled academic lecture or lab slot occurring within a timetable day.
_Avoid_: Slot, class hour, class.

**Break**:
An unscheduled interval of free time between consecutive periods lasting longer than 5 minutes.
_Avoid_: Recess, gap, interval.

**Saturday Working Day**:
A Saturday occurring on the 1st, 3rd, or 5th week of a calendar month on which academic classes are conducted.
_Avoid_: Working Saturday, class Saturday.

**Event**:
A scheduled date-specific notice (series exam, class test, deadline, reminder, or general announcement) that automatically expires at 13:30 IST on its scheduled date.
_Avoid_: Notice, ticket, alert.

**Task Completion**:
A personal confirmation by an individual student that a deadline or reminder is finished, preserved strictly in local device storage.
_Avoid_: Deletion, resolution, global complete.

**Exam Mode**:
A timetable display mode triggered manually by an admin or automatically when an exam is scheduled within 24 hours, replacing the standard timetable with the exam schedule.
_Avoid_: Test mode, assessment view.
